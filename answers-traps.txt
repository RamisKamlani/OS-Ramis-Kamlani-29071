Q1. Which registers contain arguments to functions? For example, which register holds 13 in main's call to printf?
Ans. The registers a0-a7 contain arguments passed to functions . In main's call to printf register a2 holds 13.

Q2. Where is the call to function f in the assembly code for main? Where is the call to g? (Hint: the compiler may inline functions.)
Ans . There is no call to function f or g in main assembly code. Instead of making a function call, the compiler replaced the entire expression with its final value, 12.
